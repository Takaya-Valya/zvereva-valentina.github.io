Research in progress

Foreign-Agent Designations and Political Speech in Russia

Independent research project

Abstract: This project studies how highly visible political repression changes the public positions of targeted individuals and organizations. Using Russia's foreign-agent designations and online speech, I examine whether stigmatizing legal labels deter dissent or instead generate countervailing effects through visibility, recognition, and credibility among regime opponents.

Missing Markets and the Boundaries of the Firm

Ongoing research

Abstract: Why do firms in lower-income economies internalize activities that similar firms in richer economies outsource? This project studies how thin supplier markets, contracting and enforcement frictions, and missing infrastructure shape firms' choices between producing tasks in-house, outsourcing them, or dropping them altogether.

Talent Discovery and Hiring Practices

Ongoing research

Abstract: This project examines why firms use different approaches to discovering talent: intensive screening before hire versus broader hiring followed by learning on the job. The goal is to measure firm-specific hiring practices in worker career data and study when talent discovery can become a source of competitive advantage.

Research collaborations

Journalists, Ideology, and Media Markets

Research collaboration

Research on journalists' careers and ideological positioning using employment histories, news text, and outlet-level data. My work includes large-scale author matching, position classification, mover analysis, data validation, and text-based ideology measurement.

Digital Transformation and Reskilling

Research collaboration

Research on how workers and organizations respond to technological change in a large-bank setting. The project links technology adoption and organizational interventions to worker productivity, task allocation, reskilling, and adjustment within teams.
