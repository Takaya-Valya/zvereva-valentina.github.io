Valentina Zvereva

I am a Predoctoral Research Professional at the University of Chicago Booth School of Business, where I work with Jacob Conway and Virginia Minni.

My research interests are in industrial organization, political economy, and applied microeconomics. I am particularly interested in how firms organize production and talent, how institutions shape economic behavior, and how information and political environments affect individuals and organizations.

Before joining Chicago Booth, I worked on research projects at CEMFI, Columbia Business School, and the Central Bank of Russia. You can learn more about my research here and find my CV here.
